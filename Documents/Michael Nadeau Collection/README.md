# The Michael Nadeau Collection

These documents come from the personal collection of **Michael Nadeau**, donated for
public preservation in September 2026.

Michael Nadeau is the former editor-in-chief of *80 Micro* and *HOT CoCo* magazines, and
the former executive editor of *BYTE*. He has worked for or contributed to many other
computer and technology publications, among them *CSO*, *Data Informed*, *SAP Experts* and
*Family PC*, and he is the author of *Collectible Microcomputers* and the *BYTE Guide to
CD-ROM*. Now retired from full-time editing, he volunteers with local energy-related
organizations and writes for cybersecurity publications.

This material was gathered over the years he spent covering Tandy and Radio Shack from the
editor's desk.

## What's here

387 files, roughly 650 MB, dating from 1958 to 1993 with the bulk falling between 1983
and 1988. The collection is overwhelmingly Tandy and Radio Shack corporate material:

- Press releases and press kits, including the original 1977 Model I and 1979 Model II kits
- Product brochures and fliers
- Magazine advertisements clipped from *Byte*, *Inc.*, *Business Week*, *Parents* and others
- Radio Shack computer catalogs, 1981 through 1988
- Tandy Corporation annual reports (1977, 1985) and corporate brochures
- Issues of the Radio Shack Microcomputer Newsletter
- A handful of manuals and service documentation

Filenames follow Michael's original convention, which begins with the year and describes
the document.
